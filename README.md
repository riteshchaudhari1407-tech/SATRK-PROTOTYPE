# Antigravity Scam Guard (CS-1) - SIH 2026

## 1. Problem Understanding
Digital Arrest frauds and fake authority impersonation (CBI, Police, Customs) are rising rapidly in India. Scammers use extreme urgency and legal threats over video calls to trick citizens into transferring money. Our project provides an AI-powered real-time detection and explainability shield.

## 2. Our Approach & Architecture
We use a Modular Monolith architecture combining:
- **Layer 1:** Fast Rule-Based Engine (`scam_keywords.json`) for instant keyword flagging.
- **Layer 2:** ML/NLP text processing.
- **Layer 3:** RAG-powered I4C advisory search to provide legal explanations on why a message is a scam.
